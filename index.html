How It Works

Our streamlined process makes it easy to recover your lost belongings or help others find theirs.

01

Report

Create a detailed listing of the item you lost or found, including photos and location data.

02

Search

Browse the portal or use our smart search to look through all recently reported items on campus.

03

Match

Connect securely through the platform when a lost item matches a found listing.

04

Return

Meet at a designated safe campus spot to verify ownership and return the item.
